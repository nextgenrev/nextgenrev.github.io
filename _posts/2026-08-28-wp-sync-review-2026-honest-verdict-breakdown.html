What Is WP Sync?

Before diving deeper into this WP Sync Review, let us cover the basics.

WP Sync is a WordPress plugin that automatically captures leads from ads, forms, webinars and pushes them to over 150 apps without Zapier.



It eliminates manual CSV exports, delayed follow‑ups, and the Zapier tax by auto‑syncing contacts in five‑minute intervals.

Unique Value Proposition: Auto‑sync every five minutes, unlimited contacts and automations, and a built‑in bridge to 150+ apps without extra fees.
